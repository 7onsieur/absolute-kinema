# Example brief: The water cycle (13 s)

**Topic:** the water cycle. **Audience:** secondary-school or first-year students.
**Remember:** water moves in a loop: evaporation → condensation → precipitation → collection.

Narration:
1. Water is always on the move.
2. The sun heats the sea, and water evaporates into the air.
3. It cools into clouds, falls as rain, and flows back to the sea.

Visuals: title with a water drop → a four-step flow (sun, cloud, rain, sea) that builds
as each step is spoken, closed by a looping arrow.
Theme: day. No recording supplied (timing is estimated, captions are shown on screen).
