# Animation brief

**Topic:** How earthquakes happen
**Audience:** secondary-school students / curious general audience, no prior geology
**One thing they should remember:** plates lock, stress builds, rock snaps, and the released energy travels as seismic waves we feel as shaking.
**Length:** 20–30 seconds (requested by user; longer than the skill's 10–15 s default)

## Narration (write it as you would say it)
1. How do earthquakes happen?
2. Earth's outer shell is broken into giant pieces called tectonic plates, which drift a few centimetres every year.
3. Where two plates meet, their edges lock together, and stress slowly builds up in the rock.
4. When the rock finally snaps, the plates lurch past each other, releasing energy in a split second.
5. That energy travels outwards as seismic waves, and the shaking we feel is an earthquake.
